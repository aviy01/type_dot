Why this exists

A few principles the project is built around

◆

No accounts, no ads

Open the file and type. Nothing to sign up for, nothing tracking you in the background.

▲

Letter-balanced practice

Pangram mode distributes every letter roughly evenly, instead of favoring common letters like every other test.

●

Actually useful feedback

A ranked breakdown of exactly which characters you mistype, not just a single score.
